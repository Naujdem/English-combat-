# Currículo A1 de English Combat

Guía de contenidos para el nivel A1. Todo el contenido del juego (preguntas, charlas, escenarios) debe salir de aquí.

## 1. Objetivos generales (lo que el jugador debe poder hacer al terminar A1)

- Presentarse y dar información personal básica.
- Hablar de su familia, casa, escuela y rutinas diarias.
- Entender y usar frases muy simples sobre necesidades inmediatas.
- Preguntar y responder sobre cosas concretas (dónde vive, qué tiene, qué le gusta…).
- Interactuar de forma sencilla si el interlocutor habla despacio.

## 2. Gramática A1 (orden de aparición)

| # | Punto gramatical | Uso principal | Ejemplo correcto | Error típico del enemigo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verb to be (am/is/are) | Identidad, descripción, estados | I am a student. She is happy. | I is a student. / She are happy. |
| 2 | Pronombres personales + posesivos | Sujeto y posesión | My name is Ana. This is her book. | Me name is Ana. / This is she book. |
| 3 | Artículos (a/an/the) | Presentar objetos | I have a cat. The cat is black. | I have cat. / A cat is black. |
| 4 | There is / There are | Existencia de cosas | There is a book on the table. | There have a book. / There is books. |
| 5 | Present Simple (afirmativo) | Rutinas y hechos | She goes to school every day. | She go to school. |
| 6 | Present Simple (negativo y preguntas) | Negar y preguntar rutinas | He doesn't like coffee. Does she live here? | He not like coffee. / Does she lives? |
| 7 | Can / Can't | Habilidad y permiso | I can swim. Can you help me? | I can to swim. / You can helps me? |
| 8 | Preposiciones de lugar | Ubicación | The book is on the table. | The book is in the table. |
| 9 | This / That / These / Those | Señalar objetos | This is my bag. Those are my books. | This are my books. |
| 10 | Plurales (regulares + irregulares comunes) | Contar objetos | One child → two children | Two childs / Two child |
| 11 | Adverbios de frecuencia (básicos) | Frecuencia de rutinas | I always get up at 7. | I get up always at 7. |
| 12 | Imperativos básicos | Órdenes e instrucciones | Open the door. Don't talk. | Opens the door. |
| 13 | Like / Love / Hate + sustantivo / -ing | Gustos | I like pizza. I love reading. | I like eat pizza. |

## 3. Vocabulario esencial por temas

- **Información personal:** name, age, country, nationality, job, address, phone number, email
- **Familia:** mother, father, brother, sister, parents, grandparents, son, daughter, husband, wife
- **Escuela / aula:** teacher, student, classroom, book, pen, pencil, desk, chair, board, bag, homework, subject (Maths, English, History…)
- **Casa:** house, flat/apartment, room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, living room, garden, door, window, table, chair, bed
- **Comida y bebida:** breakfast, lunch, dinner, water, milk, coffee, tea, bread, rice, chicken, fish, apple, banana, pizza, sandwich
- **Ropa y colores:** T-shirt, shirt, trousers, dress, shoes, jacket, hat · red, blue, green, yellow, black, white, brown, pink
- **Números, tiempo y calendario:** numbers 1–100, days of the week, months, seasons, time (o'clock, half past, quarter past/to)
- **Rutinas diarias:** get up, have breakfast, go to school/work, study, have lunch, play, watch TV, go to bed
- **Gustos y tiempo libre:** play football/tennis, listen to music, watch films, read books, go shopping
- **Lugares de la ciudad:** school, park, shop, supermarket, café, restaurant, hospital, bank, street
- **Otros:** animals/pets (dog, cat, bird), body parts (head, eyes, nose, mouth, arms, legs), adjetivos básicos (big, small, happy, sad, good, bad, hot, cold, new, old)

## 4. Funciones comunicativas

Saludar y despedirse · presentarse y presentar a otros · dar y pedir información personal · hablar de la familia · describir la casa y objetos · hablar de la rutina diaria · expresar gustos y preferencias · pedir comida y bebida · hablar de habilidad (can/can't) · preguntar y dar la hora · dar direcciones muy simples · describir personas de forma básica.
